TRANSPARENCY FIRST

Your privacy.
Clearly explained.

How the Kujaku project website and Android application interact with information.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the Kujaku project website and the Kujaku Android application distributed from the official project repository. Kujaku is an independent open-source project. The website does not require an account to browse public information or download releases.

2. Website data

The website is designed as a public information and download site. It does not provide user registration, advertising profiles, or a first-party account system. The hosting provider (Vercel) and infrastructure providers may process standard technical request data, such as IP address, browser details, and request logs, to deliver and secure the site. Review the provider's privacy documentation for its retention and processing practices.

3. Android app and third-party services

Kujaku connects to third-party services and content sources to provide optional functionality. Depending on the features you use, those services may receive information necessary to fulfil your request. Examples include AniList account authorization and synchronization, and requests to external anime or manga sources. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Kujaku does not host the anime or manga content returned by external sources. When you open a third-party source, your connection may be made directly to that provider, which can process your IP address, device/browser information, and request data.

4. Account credentials and local data

Authentication and account integrations are handled by their respective providers. Do not share access tokens, passwords, or private account data in public issue reports. App preferences and library information may be stored locally on your device. Exact behavior can vary by integration and build configuration.

5. Diagnostics and optional integrations

Builds may differ in included integrations and diagnostics. This website does not claim that every build uses identical analytics or crash-reporting components. Check the permissions and privacy disclosures that accompany the specific build you install, and review the app's settings for available privacy controls.

6. Data sharing and retention

The Kujaku website does not sell personal information. Data processed by GitHub, Vercel, AniList, external sources, or other third-party services is governed by those providers' policies. Kujaku cannot control their retention periods or security practices.

7. Children's privacy

Kujaku is not designed to collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise use of third-party content sources and services.

8. Security

Use official project links, keep your device updated, and install only APKs whose source and signing identity you trust. Treat third-party extensions and websites with care.

9. Your choices

You can choose whether to use optional integrations, revoke third-party authorization through the relevant provider, manage app permissions in Android settings, or stop using the website and application. Requests about data held by a third-party provider should be directed to that provider.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Kujaku evolves. The latest version will be published on this page. For privacy questions or project concerns, contact the maintainer through the official GitHub repository.

This policy is project information, not legal advice. The exact data practices of third-party integrations are controlled by their respective providers.
